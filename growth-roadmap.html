05 / Questions
Before you begin

What club owners usually ask first.

Is the Padel Club Growth Roadmap free?

Yes. The assessment and preliminary snapshot are complimentary. Detailed diagnosis, recommendations and implementation support are part of Julian's consulting engagements.

Will everyone receive a complimentary Expert Review?

Reviews are offered to qualified applicants where Julian believes a working conversation can create value. The application protects the quality of the review and ensures the relevant decision makers are involved.

Can I use the Roadmap before my club opens?

Yes. Choose Preparing to Open. It examines the commercial and operating decisions that are most expensive to reverse later.

Can I purchase a Focused Operator Sprint directly?

Not as a generic package. Julian recommends a sprint only when the diagnosis shows one clearly defined constraint can be addressed without ignoring a more important underlying issue.

Are the prices fixed?

The published figures are starting prices. Final investment reflects scope, club stage, stakeholders, available data, travel and the level of implementation support.

Does Julian work internationally?

Yes. Julian is available for select projects worldwide. Remote, on-site and hybrid delivery depend on the engagement.
